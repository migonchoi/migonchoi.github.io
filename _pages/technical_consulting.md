I help researchers and technical teams turn complex scientific questions into structured, actionable projects.

### How I can help

**AI/ML for experimental research**  
Choosing appropriate modeling approaches for small, noisy, or heterogeneous experimental datasets.

**Experimental data strategy**  
Structuring data, defining meaningful targets, and identifying what additional experiments would be most informative.

**Research problem formulation**  
Turning broad technical questions into tractable research problems, hypotheses, and decision frameworks.

**AI-enabled experimental workflows**  
Identifying where machine learning, active learning, or automation can meaningfully improve an experimental workflow.

**Technical landscape research**  
Synthesizing scientific literature, technologies, and emerging approaches to support research or strategic decisions.

### Example projects

- Evaluate whether machine learning is appropriate for an experimental dataset
- Design an active-learning strategy for selecting the next experiments
- Review and improve an experimental data pipeline
- Structure an AI-for-science research project
- Map technologies and research trends in an emerging scientific field
- Translate experimental challenges into computational or data-driven workflows

### Areas I know particularly well

Materials science, semiconductor materials, energy materials, experimental science, machine learning for scientific data, Bayesian modeling, uncertainty quantification, active learning, and autonomous experimentation.
