I work with students and early-career researchers who are navigating graduate school, research careers, and transitions across science, engineering, and AI.

### How I can help

**Graduate school strategy**  
Identifying programs, research directions, and application strategies that fit your interests and long-term goals.

**Research positioning**  
Clarifying your research story and communicating what makes your background distinctive.

**CV and application materials**  
Reviewing CVs, research statements, cover letters, and other research-focused application materials.

**Career transitions**  
Thinking through moves between academia, industry, research labs, and interdisciplinary fields such as AI for science.

**Research direction**  
Helping early-stage researchers turn broad interests into concrete research questions and project ideas.

### Example questions we could work through

- What kind of PhD programs or labs should I target?
- How should I position an interdisciplinary research background?
- Does my CV communicate my strengths clearly?
- How do I move from experimental science into AI-enabled research?
- Should I pursue academia, industry research, or another path?
- How can I build a stronger research profile over the next 6–12 months?
